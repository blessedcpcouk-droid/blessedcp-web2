About Our Live-In Care Services

At Blessed Caring Partnership, our Live-In Care service provides dedicated one-to-one support for people who wish to remain in the comfort of their own home while receiving full-time professional care. A trained caregiver lives in the home to offer assistance throughout the day and night, ensuring safety, companionship, and peace of mind.

Whether you require support due to ageing, illness, disability, or recovery after surgery, our personalised live-in care plans are designed to meet your unique needs. We focus on maintaining independence, dignity, and quality of life while providing compassionate care every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Live-In Care?

Live-In Care is a service where a professional caregiver lives in your home to provide 24-hour support with daily activities, personal care, and companionship.

Who can benefit from Live-In Care?

Live-In Care is suitable for older adults, people with disabilities, individuals recovering from illness or surgery, and anyone who needs continuous support at home.

Can Live-In Care be personalised?

Yes. Every care plan is tailored to meet the client's medical needs, daily routine, preferences, and lifestyle.

Do caregivers help with household tasks?

Yes. Our caregivers can assist with meal preparation, light housekeeping, laundry, shopping, medication reminders, and other daily tasks.

How do I arrange Live-In Care?

Simply contact Blessed Caring Partnership to discuss your needs. Our team will create a personalised care plan for you or your loved one.

Looking for Professional Live-In Care?

Our compassionate team is ready to provide personalised 24-hour support, helping you or your loved one live safely and comfortably at home.
