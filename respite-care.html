Frequently Asked Questions

What is Respite Care?

Respite Care provides temporary professional care so that family caregivers can take a break while their loved ones continue receiving quality support.

How long can Respite Care be provided?

Respite Care can be arranged for a few hours, overnight, weekends, or longer periods depending on your needs.

Who can benefit from Respite Care?

Anyone caring for an older adult, a person with a disability, or someone recovering from illness can benefit from respite services.

Is care personalised?

Yes. Every respite care plan is tailored to the individual's medical needs, daily routine, and personal preferences.

How do I arrange Respite Care?

Simply contact Blessed Caring Partnership, and our team will help you create a personalised respite care plan.
